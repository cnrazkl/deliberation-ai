# ADR-0034: local knowledge trial contract

Accepted for trial preparation, 5 October 2026. Human gold/model acceptance is pending.

## Decision

The owner chose individual files and accepted the [DA-119 trial protocol](../evaluation/KNOWLEDGE_EVALUATION.md)
with its [exact-plan approval](../evaluation/KNOWLEDGE_CONTRACT_APPROVAL.json).
Use owned local collections, exact grants, explicit preparation and immutable encrypted
evidence. No external notebook/service is a local prerequisite. Start with bounded
UTF-8 text/Markdown/selectable PDF; scanned/image originals remain unverified.
Defer directory/ZIP import, automatic OCR, persistent plaintext/embedding indexes,
autonomous retrieval, private-branch routing and automatic schedule refresh.

## Consequences

The first contract/fake-adapter increment is DA-120. Runtime ingestion/preparation belongs
to DA-121/122. The frozen cohort/protocol/fixture digests and blank independent review
forms establish reproducible preparation, not factual accuracy or a production connector.
Approval of trial limits is separate from human labels, measurements and rollout.
Changes to this frozen study require a new plan and matching owner review before results.
Existing council independence, minority/raw outputs and provider adapter boundaries remain.
