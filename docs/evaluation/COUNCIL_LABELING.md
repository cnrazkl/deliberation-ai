DA-052 adds an **offline structural gate** before a human-labeled source-level council corpus is assembled. It does not supply real labels, verify a source's authority, authenticate a reviewer, contact a model, or produce an accuracy claim.

Prepare four versioned JSON inputs for `assembleAdjudicatedCouncilCoverageCorpus(intake, [reviewA, reviewB], adjudication)` in `@deliberation-ai/evaluation`:

1. The `council-coverage-intake-v1` intake freezes each case's source text, `sourceId`, source reference, UTC capture time, SHA-256 of that exact UTF-8 text, question, language, risk tags and development/held-out split. `sourceId` denotes the underlying document, not just a paragraph; keep all excerpts and paraphrases from one document in one split. Record lawful source use and the original publication/capture details outside the scoring file when applicable.
2. Two `council-coverage-review-v1` sheets use different reviewer ids. Each independently records every case's material claims, criticality and exact UTF-16 evidence spans in the frozen source text. Reviewers should not see the council output, each other's sheet or held-out predictions while labeling. Their ids are labels, not identity proof.
3. A `council-coverage-adjudication-v1` record uses a third distinct id. For each case, final `goldClaims` link to the contributing review claim ids. A final claim supported by only one reviewer needs an explicit disagreement rationale. Every review claim must be used once in a final claim or explicitly rejected with a rationale. Unresolved substantive disputes should stay outside a promoted corpus until resolved, and their count should be reported.

The assembler validates source hashes, exact evidence offsets, case completeness, unique reviewer claims, reviewer/adjudicator separation, complete resolution of review claims and source-level split isolation. It returns a `council-coverage-v1` corpus with source snapshot metadata, plus SHA-256 manifests for the corpus and the full labeling inputs. Save the original four inputs alongside those hashes; a hash alone cannot recover the review trail. The assembler cannot decide whether a paraphrased claim is truly supported or whether two pseudonyms represent independent people.
